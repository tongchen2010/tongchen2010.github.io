Brain Connectome Explorer

The human brain as a network — regions grouped into the seven canonical functional networks. Drag nodes, raise the connection threshold to watch the networks separate, isolate a subnetwork, or highlight the regions most affected in Alzheimer's.

Drag nodes · scroll to zoom · click a region for its connections · click a network in the legend to isolate it
Regions are grouped into the seven canonical functional networks (Yeo et al., 2011). The connectivity here is a schematic illustration of the brain's modular, small-world, hub-organized topology — not a single subject's scan. Node size reflects each region's degree (its hub-ness). Built with D3; runs entirely in your browser.
