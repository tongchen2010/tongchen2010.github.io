Checking WebGPU support…

The model is downloaded once (a few hundred MB to ~2 GB) from a free CDN, cached in your browser, then runs on your own GPU. The first load is the slow part; after that it is instant.

WebGPU is not available in this browser 😕

This demo needs WebGPU to run the model on your graphics card. Try:

  • The latest Chrome or Edge on desktop (best support).
  • On Linux you may need to launch with --enable-unsafe-webgpu.
  • Check your status at webgpureport.org.
