%========================= RESEARCH INTERESTS =========================
\section{Research Interests}
Machine learning and AI for medical imaging, with a focus on Alzheimer's disease and Lewy
body dementia. My research develops self-supervised representation learning methods for
longitudinal brain MRI, models disease progression across the cognitive impairment
continuum, and builds interpretable models on large multi-modal clinical cohorts.
