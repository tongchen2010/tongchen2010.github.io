Tong Chen

Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science, The University of Texas at Arlington

Department of Computer Science and Engineering

The University of Texas at Arlington

Arlington, TX

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at The University of Texas at Arlington, advised by Dr. Dajiang Zhu. I expect to graduate in December 2026.

My research develops machine learning methods for neuroimaging and neurodegenerative disease — particularly Alzheimer’s disease and Lewy body dementia, spanning the full continuum from mild cognitive impairment to dementia. I focus on:

  • Graph-based representations of brain structure and connectivity (graph neural networks on cortical surfaces and connectomes)
  • Disease-progression modeling — continuous staging frameworks that place patients along the cognitive impairment continuum
  • Interpretable, multi-cohort clinical analysis using large neuroimaging datasets (ADNI, NACC, and in-house LBD cohorts)

My work has appeared in Alzheimer’s & Dementia, MICCAI, and IEEE ISBI, including a first-author MICCAI 2025 paper on a unified continuous staging framework for AD and LBD via hierarchical anatomical features.

Before UTA, I received my M.S. and B.S. in Computer Science from Kennesaw State University, where I worked on computer vision and deep learning with Dr. Junggab Son and Dr. Jiho Noh.

I am on the job market for faculty and postdoctoral positions starting in 2027. Feel free to reach out!
